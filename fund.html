§ 03 — Investment Fund

Our Strategy.

GIC manages a diversified, multi-asset portfolio combining bottom-up security selection with top-down macroeconomic analysis across seven specialized investment desks.

Investment Approach

Members employ a multi-faceted investment strategy involving quantitative and qualitative analysis to produce investment pitches and construct a diversified portfolio. Pitches include a presentation of theses supported by various financial models, including discounted cash flow analysis, leveraged buyouts, merger models, and dividend discount models.

The unique layout of GIC's investment groups mirrors the format of leading financial institutions, promoting exposure to multiple asset classes. Each desk is structured with a Desk Head who oversees prospective investments and facilitates team education.

Following each pitch, active members and the Portfolio Committee vote on whether the investment should be included in the club portfolio. All profits are reinvested into the fund or returned to members in the form of events or investing capital.

Investment Strategies
01
Global Macro

Investments based on global macroeconomic outlooks and understanding of how world events affect financial markets. Members analyze systematic risks to increase returns through weekly news discussions and an emphasis on using current global events to create theses.

02
Event-Driven

Identifying and capitalizing on specific corporate events — mergers, spin-offs, restructurings, earnings catalysts, and regulatory changes — that create temporary mispricings in securities across asset classes.

03
Multi-Strategy Long/Short

Members develop pitches across a variety of asset classes and industries while being encouraged to explore different long and short plays, building comprehensive analytical skills and a nuanced view of risk.

Investment Desks
DESK 01
U.S. Equities

Covers publicly traded companies headquartered in the U.S. traded on American exchanges, while remaining attentive to broader global economic forces that shape domestic performance.

DESK 02
High Yield Debt

Covers high-yield bonds and credit securities trading below par. Members gain experience analyzing distressed companies with low liquidity and synthesizing different recovery scenarios within the capital structure.

DESK 03
Real Estate

Takes a deep look into private real estate properties and publicly traded REITs across traditional and niche asset classes — a great way to explore an alternative asset class and its diverse career opportunities.

DESK 04
Global Equities

Targets companies based outside the U.S. in developed regions representing over half the largest global public companies. Explores unique political, sovereign, economic, and foreign exchange risks.

DESK 05
Special Situations

Focuses on investment opportunities not covered by other desks, including Forex, Derivatives, Commodities, and Private Equity — providing insight into alternative and unique investment strategies and mechanics.

DESK 06
Emerging Markets

Focuses on funding investment opportunities across global markets on the rise. Teaches members to seek opportunities in areas with cheap valuations and greater growth prospects while analyzing geopolitical trends and currency stability.

DESK 07
Technology

Covers companies within the technology industry. Analysts gain insight on how to value high-growth companies with unique business models and industry-specific metrics in a rapidly evolving sector.
