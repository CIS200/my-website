§ 06 — Apply

Join GIC.

Each semester, GIC recruits undergraduate students with diverse backgrounds from all colleges at the University of Michigan who have a passion for learning. No prior investing experience or knowledge is required.​

Recruitment Process

You do not need to be a finance major or have prior investing experience to apply to GIC. We are looking for students who are curious, thoughtful, and genuinely interested in markets. What matters most is your willingness to learn and contribute.

Who Can Apply

All U-M undergraduates

Applications Open

Each semester

Timeline

~2 week process

APPLY NOW → INTEREST FORM →
01

Written Application

Submit your resume, a short personal statement, and answers to application questions.

02

First-Round Interview

Behavioral and fit-based interview with current members to assess interest, curiosity, and potential.

03

Second-Round Interview

A more in-depth interview designed to assess how you think, communicate, and approach investment-related questions.
